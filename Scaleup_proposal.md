# 1,000-Company Scale-Up Sourcing Framework

## Overview
To scale from a 25-company sample to a list of 1,000 high-quality ICP companies, a structured sourcing pipeline is required. Based on typical B2B data filtering results, i have estimated a conversion rate of around **25%**, meaning roughly **4,000 initial company records** will be needed to generate **1,000 validated targets**.

---

## Implementation Plan

### Week 1: Data Collection
- Gathering company data from sources such as Apollo, Tracxn, industrial directories, and public business databases.
- Applying basic filters: Location: India, Revenue: ₹50Cr–₹500Cr, Industries: Precision Engineering, Electronics, Aerospace, Defense, and Life Sciences

### Week 2: Automated Qualification
- Using Python scripts to scrape company websites, Analyzing sections like **About Us** and **Products**, Excluding distributors, traders, brokers, and consulting firms using keyword-based filtering.

### Week 3: Data Enrichment & Scoring
- Enrich company records using public job postings and professional platforms, Look for signals such as: SAP/ERP implementation roles, Operations leadership positions, Scaling and transformation initiatives, Use these signals to score and prioritize companies.

### Week 4: Quality Review
- Manually reviewing borderline cases, Verify ownership structure, operational status, and company fit, Finalize and categorize the top 1,000 companies into priority groups.
---
## Alternative Sourcing Channels

1. **DSIR Directory**
   - Identifies companies with recognized in-house R&D facilities.

2. **Indian Patent Office (InPASS)**
   - Helps find companies with active patents and innovation activity.

3. **Industrial Expo Exhibitor Lists**
   - Sources companies participating in events such as Aero India, IMTEX, and Pharma Pro&Pack.

---

### Expected Outcome
A clean and validated database of 1,000 high-potential manufacturing and industrial companies, prioritized for outreach and further analysis.
